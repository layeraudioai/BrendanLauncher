BRENDANLAUNCHER
-
for media-machine place music in BrendanLauncher/apps/media-machine/content/ then run build.bat in BrendanLauncher/apps/media-machine/ without media-machine running on NS2, you do not need to reset the server, just close media-machine and leave BrendanLauncher on the NS2 running then run build.bat for media-machine then open media-machine on the NS2 again, you might need to try booting it from BrendanLauncher on the NS2 twice
-
GUIDE:
-
1st go into BrendanLauncher/ and double click build-aio.bat
-
2nd be patient
-
3rd once it asks your ip look at the displayed adapter and put the 10.0.0.X for your used adapter
-
4th make a pairing pin
-
5th on your ns2 run ns2jb exploit then the V=START(39380,10,0,0,X,P,I,N,#) line displayed on pc as V=START(39380,10,0,0,X,`*`,`*`,`*`,`*`)
-
6th be patient while the pc does its stuff
-
7th you are in the BrendanLauncher menu on your NS2
-
8th next time just run BrendanLauncher.bat not BrendanLauncher/build-aio.bat unless you change the apps in BrendanLauncher/apps/ or change the BrendanLauncher source
